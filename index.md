## 🧰 Troubleshooting Made Simple

**Issue:** *Windows shows "Windows protected your PC"*  
**Fix:** Click "More info" → "Run anyway". This is a standard notice for unsigned apps, not a virus warning.



**Issue:** *Sounds won't play*  
**Fix:** Check that your computer's volume is on and that you haven't muted QuietField in the system volume mixer (right-click the speaker icon in your taskbar → "Open Volume Mixer").  



**Issue:** *The app won't open at all*  
**Fix:** Most likely, the file didn't finish downloading. Delete the file, revisit the **[download link]**(https://github.com/DLStudios-del/QuietField), and try again. If the issue persists, try right-clicking the installer and selecting "Run as administrator."



**Issue:** *The sound skips or crackles*  
**Fix:** Close any heavy programs running in the background (like video games or video editors). If problems continue, restart your computer and try again.
